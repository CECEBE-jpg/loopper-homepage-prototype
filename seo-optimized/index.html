      What is Loopper?

      Loopper is a B2B promotional products supplier based in Amsterdam, the Netherlands. Businesses worldwide order custom promotional products, branded merchandise and corporate gifts from a range of more than 200,000 items, each personalised with a logo, text or design. Founded by Edwin den Hartog, Loopper combines a large online catalogue with free advice from Promotional Products Consultants.

      How do you order custom promotional products?

      1. Request a quote

        Choose a product or describe your idea, add the quantity and your logo. You receive a quote within 30 minutes at most.

      2. Approve your free mockup

        Within 1 hour you see your logo on the product as a free digital mockup. Ask for changes until it is right.

      3. Receive your order

        After your approval the products are produced and delivered to the address you choose.

      What is included in the price of promotional products?

      The price you see includes printing and design costs, calculated for a realistic order quantity. You know exactly what your custom promotional products will cost before you order, with no hidden costs. Need a different quantity or print method? Ask for a quote for your exact order.

      Questions about custom promotional products

      What are custom promotional products?

      Custom promotional products are everyday items, such as pens, water bottles, tote bags or clothing, printed or engraved with a company's logo. Businesses use them to build brand awareness, thank clients and reward employees.

      Where can I buy promotional products with my logo?

      Loopper is a promotional products supplier based in Amsterdam that supplies businesses worldwide. You can choose from more than 200,000 items online, receive a quote within 30 minutes and see a free digital mockup within 1 hour.

      How fast can I get a quote for branded merchandise?

      At Loopper you receive a quote within 30 minutes at most after sending your request, and a free digital mockup within 1 hour.

      Are printing costs included in the price?

      Yes. Loopper prices include printing and design costs for a realistic order quantity, so there are no hidden costs. You receive an exact quote for your quantity.

      Can I see the design before production?

      Yes. Loopper prepares a free digital mockup within 1 hour, and production only starts after you approve the design.

      Can Loopper make a product that is not in the catalogue?

      Yes. The Loopper Special Projects team develops custom products with a global network of manufacturing partners, from concept and prototype to production and delivery.

      Which countries does Loopper deliver to?

      Loopper supplies businesses worldwide from its base in Europe, with local market knowledge in the Netherlands, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Poland and the United Kingdom.

      What is the minimum order quantity for promotional products at Loopper?

      The minimum order at Loopper is 1 to 25 pieces, depending on the product. Each product page shows its lowest quantity and the all-in price per piece.

      How much do promotional products with a logo cost?

      Loopper prices include printing and design costs. At 250 pieces, a double-walled stainless steel cup (250 ml) costs €3.60 per piece and a stainless steel thermo flask (500 ml) €5.47 per piece.
